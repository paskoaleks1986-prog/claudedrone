Features:
**Advanced Technology for Aerial Precision**
The AirSelfie 45A 4 in 1 brushless electronic speed controller (ESC) is a cutting-edge component for small unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) and crossing drones. Engineered with a 4 in 1 integrated design, this ESC simplifies the structural design of the aircraft arm, thereby improving the assembly efficiency of the whole machine. The lightweight build at just 12.5g not only reduces the overall weight of the drone but also enhances its flexibility, allowing for more dynamic maneuvers and precise control.
**Optimized Performance for UAV Enthusiasts**
Equipped with an 8-bit high-speed MCU, the AirSelfie 45A ESC boasts ultra-fast speed and excellent performance, making it an upgraded alternative for consumer-grade UAVs. The controller supports high frequency PWM, ensuring smooth and responsive control. Hardware synchronous rectification and regenerative braking contribute to higher battery energy recovery efficiency, enhancing the drone's overall efficiency and endurance. With its superior throttle linearity and sensitive deceleration response, the ESC elevates the drone's flexibility, providing a more responsive and agile flying experience.
**Versatile Application for Aerial Adventures**
Whether you're capturing aerial footage or navigating through challenging terrains, the AirSelfie 45A ESC is designed to meet the demands of small aerial drones and crossing unmanned aerial vehicles. Its compact size and lightweight design make it an ideal choice for hobbyists and enthusiasts looking to upgrade their UAVs for improved performance and reliability. The ESC's compatibility with a wide range of small UAVs ensures that it can be easily integrated into various projects, making it a versatile and essential component for those passionate about aerial exploration.
